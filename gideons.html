Gideon's Inferno

A satirical descent into bureaucratic hell.

About the Book

When burned-out Navy vet Gideon Cross signs a lease with Faust Luxury Residences, he unknowingly enters a binding contract with the absurd and infernal. As floors rearrange themselves, mailboxes whisper secrets, and customer support agents offer no escape, Gideon must find a loophole in the fine print of Hell itself.
