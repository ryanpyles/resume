Shift Happens

A psychological thriller where reality fractures at 6:17 PM.

About the Book

Jack, Oren, and Damon each take a detour at exactly 6:17 PM—plunging them into a warped version of reality where time distorts, identities double, and death may already have happened. Shift Happens is a layered psychological thriller told from multiple fractured perspectives.
